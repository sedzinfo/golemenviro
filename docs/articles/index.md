# Articles

### Charts in R

- [Gauges and time
  series](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemenviro/articles/golemenviro.md):

  Download Pimoroni Enviro sensor readings from Prometheus in R and draw
  them as interactive gauges and time series.

- [Daily pattern and
  comfort](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemenviro/articles/daily-pattern.md):

  The hourly averages of the Enviro sensors in R: a heatmap of the daily
  pattern, a typical day and how comfortable the room was.

- [Air
  quality](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemenviro/articles/air-quality.md):

  Compare the particles measured by the Enviro sensor with the air
  quality guidelines of the World Health Organization (2021) in R.
